Our Team

Meet the individuals working to support farmers and strengthen our agricultural communities.

Eli Cohen

Executive Director

Eli’s big love is learning about the science of sustainable agriculture and plant health. Some of the highlights of his farming journey include volunteering at Farm in the Dell of the Red River Valley and the Folkways Farmer’s Market as well as studying at the Northern Crop Institute. He founded Dakota Harvest of Hope to help address some of the feelings of isolation and loneliness his colleagues shared with him while they were out in the fields during the planting season. Eli’s favorite time of year is August, when he gets to help with the potato harvest.

Contact: eli@dakotaharvestofhope.org

Benjamin Thoreson

Financial & Programs Director

Ben has a passion for farming and business, with both of his grandfathers being farmers in North Dakota. As a result, he has been involved with the agriculture his whole life and understands the associated struggles.

Contact: ben@dakotaharvestofhope.org

Isaac Syverson

Volunteer Coordinator

Isaac has lived in the midwest all his life, and with his familial roots in farming, he has always enjoyed farm work. He is an avid supporter of aid for farmer and ecologcal conservation.
